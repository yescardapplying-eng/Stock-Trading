Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

📅 Last Updated: October 2026

1 Introduction

Stock Trading respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data.

2 Information We Collect

We may collect your name, mobile number, state, email address, and any message you submit through our contact form. We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, provide our services, improve our website experience, and share updates related to your specific request. We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing without your consent.

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to any third party. We may share it only with trusted service providers who assist in operating our platform, or when required to do so by law.

5 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, understand how visitors use our site, and enhance our content. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.

7 Your Rights

You may request correction or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time.

8 Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please email info@stockmarket.in or call +91 12345 67890.
